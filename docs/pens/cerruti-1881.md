# Cerruti 1881

My second fountain pen was a souvenir pen from Cerruti 1881, which might be made by Laban. it took me a long while to find out it most likely from Laban. but just guess, still no 100% sure.

it sat for long time so I wanted to reuse it. but, at the time, I didn’t which cartridge to fit. so, I bought a Parker one from a local supermarket. of course, it doesn’t work. then I tried a Jinhao 2.6mm bore cartridges, which are closed but still no working. then I asked Cult Pens and a nice lady replied me that it looked like International Standard cartridges. I didn’t realise there is a long or short version. so I bought a new Waterman allure with along standard  YT it can’t fit into Cerruti one. technically it can be in but it can’t put back the barral.

eventually I gave up and bought ink syringes plus Parker Quink Black. however, it was dried out too long and it’s cannot be working even I tried many flushing. maybe other issues.

In between, I bought Parker Jotter Originals for Parker cartridges and Baoer 801 for Jinhao cartridges. so, I didn’t spend time to fix.

some days later, I bought Kaweco Perkeo and proved Cerruti is with a standard short cartridge. it’s a long story to bring me back to fountain pens.

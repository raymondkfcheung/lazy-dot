# Lamy Pens

I have a Lamy Joy and an AL-Star, as well as several fake Safari pens. Those are very cheap, and I bought them for a few reasons:

1. I can learn how to disassemble and reassemble a fountain pen. Since they are cheap, they are good for experimenting.
2. I can use them for spare parts. Most of the parts are interchangeable with the original ones.
3. I can learn how to fine-tune or grind a nib.

However, there are some catches:

1. They use 3.4 mm-bore cartridges, not Lamy cartridges.
2. Unbranded or lesser-known brands can be very poor writers. Wing Sung and Jinhao are generally good manufacturers, although not every pen they make is good. Some people also say Hongdian and Majohn are good, but I have tried them and don’t really have much to say about them. For usable pens, I would suggest buying at least Baoer, which is a sub-brand of Jinhao.

Anyway, the fake pens are mainly for experimenting, while my original Lamy pens are for writing. I wish I could get a Lamy retractable fountain pen later, but it’s quite expensive.

Many people say the Lamy 2000 is a must-have pen. Maybe I should buy one later. For now, though, I can afford a Jinhao 80. I’m not sure whether I should buy this knockoff or not. Maybe I will… maybe I won’t.

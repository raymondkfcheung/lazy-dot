# Lamy Pens

I have Lamy Joy and AL Safari. I also get several fake Safari pens,those are cheap. I bought them because :

1. I can learn how to disamble and resemble a fountain pen. as they are cheap, so it is good experiment for me.
2. I can have spare parts. most of them can interchangeably with the original ones.
3. I can learn how to fine tune or grind a nib.
   
however, there are some catches:

1. they use 3.4mm bore cartridges, no Lamy ones.
2. unbranded or less known brands are very bad at writing. Wing Sung and Jinhao are good manufacturers. but not all. someone said Hongdizn and Majohn are good too, but. I have tried them. no comments on them. so for usable pens I suggested to buy at least Baoer, which sub brand of Jinhao.

anyway, fake pens are just for experimenting. original Lamy pens are for writing. I wish I can have a Lamy retractable pen later but it’s expensive.

many said Lamy 2000 is a must have pen. maybe I shall buy it later. but I can afford to have a Jinhao 80 at this moment. I’m not sure I shall buy this knock out or not. maybe? may not be?
